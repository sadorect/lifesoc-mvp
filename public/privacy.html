Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

LifeSOC is the companion app to the book Zero Trust by Tosin Omojola. This policy explains what personal data the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

LifeSOC is operated by Sadorect Systems Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17516007), registered office 100 Brenda Road, Hartlepool TS25 1QD, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact: support@lifesoc.app

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data or use it for marketing.

Data that stays on your device

Your security-posture scores, CVE register, patch tracker, incident log, IoC scan history, AI chat history, reading position and listening progress are stored in your browser on your own device. They are not uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data, or using another device, means you will not see them there. (The text of an AI request is still sent to generate a reply, as described above.)

Cookies and similar storage

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. The app also uses your browser's local storage to save the on-device data described above, and a service worker so the app can be installed and load quickly. None of these are used for tracking, so no consent banner is needed.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Contabo GmbH (Germany): the server that hosts the app, your account and access records.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: network delivery and protection for the website, and storage for the ebook and audiobook files. Your requests pass through Cloudflare.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA): generates AI Analyst and IoC Scanner responses from the text you submit.
  • Google LLC: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms who you are. The app also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Clerk, Inc. (USA): provided sign-in for LifeSOC until we moved accounts onto our own server. Accounts held by Clerk will be deleted there once the move is complete.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of the providers above are based in, or may process data in, the United States. Where personal data leaves the UK, we rely on adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge, where the provider is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Account and book-access records: for as long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete these within 30 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days unless you sign out sooner.
  • Server logs: about six weeks.
  • Backups: encrypted server backups are kept for up to 12 months and then overwritten.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. Email support@lifesoc.app from the address on your account. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

Connections to LifeSOC are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, the sign-in cookie cannot be read by page scripts, and book media is served through short-lived signed links.

Children

LifeSOC is intended for people aged 16 and over, and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app or by email.
